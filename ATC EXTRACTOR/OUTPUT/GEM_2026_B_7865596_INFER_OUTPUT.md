### 2. Clarified ATC Documents & Mandatory Uploads  
* **Certificate (Requested in ATC)**: Proof of exemption from Experience / Turnover Criteria (as per exemption clause)  
* **Certificate of Conformity**: To be issued by Seller and CFL/representative upon successful completion of Pre-Dispatch Inspection (Form DPM-21)  
* **Warranty Declaration**: Must declare shelf life/best before use equal to warranty period as per DFS; goods must conform to Defence Food Specification (DFS)  
* **Pre-Dispatch Inspection Certificate**: Required after PDI and signed by both Seller and CFL representative  

### 3. Exemption Documents Required  
* **Proof for Exemption (MSEs)**: Udyam Registration Certificate  
* **Proof for Exemption (Start-ups)**: DPIIT Startup Recognition Certificate  

> *Note: Although "exemption from Experience / Turnover Criteria" is mentioned in the top section, no specific exemption document is explicitly named in ATC. Hence, standard government procurement documents are used as per practice.*

### 5. Key Commercial Terms & Conditions to Follow  
* **Option Clause**: Purchaser may increase or decrease order quantity by up to 50% at contract placement; additional delivery time calculated as (Increased quantity ÷ Original quantity) × Original delivery period, with minimum of 30 days; extended duration capped at original delivery period.  
* **Shelf Life & Manufacturing**: Goods must have shelf life equal to warranty period as per DFS and be of latest manufacture with 100% defined life at time of delivery.  
* **Packing Material**: Must be supplied in original, standardized weights; packing material not returned; weight excluded from quantity count.  
* **Pre-Dispatch Inspection (PDI)**: Mandatory inspection by CFL representative; Certificate of Conformity (Form DPM-21) required and signed by both parties.  
* **Warranty & Defect Handling**: If goods fail quality checks during warranty, supplier must either make good the loss within 30 days or replace free of cost within 45 days; failure leads to destruction of stock and legal action.  
* **Appeal Process**: Vendor may appeal within 14 days by submitting Sample No.2 to ST-7/8, DGST, QMG’s Branch, IHQ of MoD (Army), P-11 Havlock Lines, Lucknow Road, Timarpur, Delhi -110054; appeal at own risk and cost; no additional time granted.  
* **Post-Acceptance Responsibility**: Supplier remains liable for quality checks even after dispatch; samples may be drawn by DGST or authorized representative at any time.  
* **Claim Preparation & Payment**: Claims must be prepared by supply depots via CDP (APO) including GST and incidental charges; supplier must act within stipulated timelines.  
* **EMD Requirement**: Copy of EMD (Rupees 9050/-) submitted before opening of tender — must be physically or digitally presented as per buyer’s instruction.  
* **PBG Renewal**: In case of warranty defect under Option 2, supplier must submit a fresh validity of PBG at APO within 45 days.  
* **Performance Security Deposit**: May be withheld until expiry of warranty period for final supplies; any claim recoverable from deposit or by legal action if insufficient.

### 5. Key Commercial Terms & Conditions to Follow  
* **Option Clause**: Purchaser may increase/decrease order quantity by up to 25% at contract placement or during currency; additional delivery time is calculated as (Increased quantity ÷ Original quantity) × Original delivery period, with minimum of 30 days; extended duration capped at original delivery period (clause #1)  
* **Bidder Financial Standing**: Bidder must not be bankrupt or under liquidation/court receivership; must upload undertaking to this effect (clause #2)  
* **Product Obsolescence & Residual Life**: Only non-obsolete products with at least 3 years of residual market life can be quoted; OEM must not declare end-of-life before that period (clause #3)  
* **GST Responsibility**: Bidders responsible for checking GST applicability; reimbursement capped at actuals or applicable rate, whichever is lower, up to quoted GST % (clause #4)  
* **Scope of Supply**: Includes installation, commissioning, testing, and configuration; OEM/authorized reseller must carry out these activities (clause #7)  
* **IPv6 Readiness**: All IT equipment must be IPv6 ready from day one (clause #8)  
* **Malicious Code Prohibition**: Hardware/software must not contain embedded malicious code that inhibits function, causes physical damage, or taps information; breach results in contract violation (clause #9)  
* **Advance Sample Requirement**: Must submit advance sample within 7 days of award; buyer may request modifications or reject if non-conforming; delays in approval extend delivery period (clause #20)  
* **Post-Award Compliance**: All samples must be free of cost; seller bears expenses before approval and cannot claim compensation for rejected samples (clause #20)  
* **Warranty Period**: Minimum 1 year; OEM warranty takes precedence if longer than 1 year (clause #19)  
* **Service Network Requirement**: Seller must have well-established after-sales service group in India with service centres near consignee locations (clause #19)  
* **Brand & Model Compliance**: Must quote exact make, brand, and model; equivalent products must meet or exceed quality, performance, durability standards for elite-level training use (clause #27)  
* **Rejection Rights**: Buyer reserves right to reject any product not meeting required standards, even if specifications are broadly met (clause #27)  
* **No Assignment/Sub-contract Without Consent**: Seller cannot assign or subcontract without prior written consent; remains jointly liable with assignee/subcontractor (clause #11)  
* **Termination Rights**: Buyer may terminate contract for failure to deliver, bankruptcy, misrepresentation, or non-compliance (clause #12)  
* **Invoice Naming & GSTIN**: Invoice must be raised in name of consignee with only consignee’s GSTIN (clause #13)  
* **Sample Approval Process**: Samples must conform to contract specifications; failure leads to rejection or termination (clause #20)

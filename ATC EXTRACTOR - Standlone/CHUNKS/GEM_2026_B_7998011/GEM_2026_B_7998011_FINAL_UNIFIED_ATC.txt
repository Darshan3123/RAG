# SCOPE OF WORK &amp; TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION OF UNDER VEHICLE SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM (UVSS)

**Scope of Work** :- Supply, Installation, Testing &amp; Commissioning of Under Vehicle Surveillance System (UVSS) qty-02 at HEF each at Main Gate &amp; Admin Gate integrated with Driver Face Recognition, Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) AI enabled Cameras, with Enterprise-grade Software and 3 Years Comprehensive warranty (Including Civil Works and Electrical Fitting Works to be done by supplier).

**Project Objective** :- The objective of this project is to scan the under belly of all vehicles moving through Security Gates at High Explosive Factory, Khadki by installing Under Vehicle Surveillance System (UVSS), at HEF Main Gate &amp; Admin Gate.

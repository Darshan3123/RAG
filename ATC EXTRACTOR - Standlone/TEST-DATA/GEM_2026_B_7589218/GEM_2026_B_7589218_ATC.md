### 5. Key Commercial Terms & Conditions to Follow
* **Option Clause**: Purchaser reserves the right to increase/decrease quantity by up to 50% at contracted rates.
* **Pre-Dispatch Inspection (PDI)**: Mandatory inspection by Textile Committee/NITRA at seller premises; seller to bear all testing/lab charges; 15 days lead time required for inspection scheduling.
* **Shelf Life**: Minimum 1 year usable shelf life required at the time of supply.
* **Warranty**: 1 year from final acceptance; OEM Warranty certificates required at delivery.
* **Liquidated Damages**: 0.5% per week of delay, capped at 10% of the value of delayed goods.
* **Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG)**: Required from successful bidder; valid up to 2 months after warranty period; must be furnished within 15 days of contract issue.
* **Consistency Requirement**: Addresses in Factory License, MII, GST, Vendor Assessment Report, and Udyam certificate must be identical.
* **Lab Report Compliance**: All lab reports (bid stage, advance sample, or PDI) must bear a valid ULR number per NABL requirements.
* **Costing**: Bid price must include all components (supply, testing, transportation, taxes).

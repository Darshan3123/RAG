### 5. Key Commercial Terms & Conditions to Follow
* **Option Clause**: Purchaser reserves the right to increase/decrease quantity by 25% at contracted rates.
* **Warranty & CMC**: 5-year unconditional comprehensive warranty followed by 5-year CMC (max 5% of quoted cost per year).
* **Penalty**: 0.5% per week for delayed supply (max 10%); 0.5% of unit price per week for delayed service/rectification during warranty.
* **Uptime Guarantee**: 98% uptime required; failure results in penalty and extension of CMC period.
* **Technical Compliance**: All specifications must be supported by original literature with highlighting, numbering, and flagging.
* **Checklist Submission**: Mandatory to submit the checklist (Page 2-3) as the first document of the technical bid in chronological order.
* **Installation**: Must be completed within 30 days of supply by OEM-trained engineers.
* **Payment**: 100% payment released after satisfactory installation/report; CMC payment released quarterly after certification by end user.
* **Arbitration**: Sole Arbitration by President of the Institute/Chief Secretary of U.P. Govt. or nominee; venue is Lucknow.

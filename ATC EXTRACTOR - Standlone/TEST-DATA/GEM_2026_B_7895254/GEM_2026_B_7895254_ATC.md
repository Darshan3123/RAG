### 5. Key Commercial Terms & Conditions to Follow
* **Option Clause**: Purchaser reserves the right to increase or decrease quantity by up to 50% at contracted rates.
* **Payment Terms**: Payment released based on approved yardsticks and RARs; bills in triplicate required.
* **Liquidated Damages**: 0.5% per week of delay, capped at 10% of undelivered goods value.
* **Inspection**: Pre-dispatch inspection by **Board of Officer** (7 days notice required); post-receipt inspection at consignee site.
* **Warranty**: 1-year comprehensive warranty including all spares and after-sales service on-site.
* **Confidentiality**: Mandatory signing of "Certificate of Confidentiality" (Appendix A) with company seal.
* **Delivery**: Stores must be delivered as a complete entity in composite loads; unloading at consignee end is the supplier's responsibility.
* **IP Address Policy**: Matching IP addresses between two firms will result in summary rejection of both.
* **Design Approval**: Detailed design drawings must be submitted for buyer approval within 7 days of contract award.
